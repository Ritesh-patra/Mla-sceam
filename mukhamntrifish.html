Empowering Fisheries Development

The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a flagship scheme for focused and sustainable development of the fisheries sector in India with an estimated investment of ₹20,050 crores.

Financial Support

Up to 40% subsidy

Technical Support

Training & guidance

Key Features of the Scheme

Enhanced Production

Increase fish production and productivity through expansion, intensification, diversification and productive utilization of land and water.

Modern Technology

Introduction of new technologies including biofloc, RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System), and cage culture for intensive fish farming.

Livelihood Support

Providing livelihood and nutritional support for socio-economically backward, women, and SC/ST communities involved in fisheries.

Available Schemes

Various schemes are available under PMMSY to support different aspects of fisheries development with attractive subsidy components.

Marine Fisheries Development

Support for deep-sea fishing vessels and equipment with up to 40% subsidy for general category and 60% for SC/ST/Women.

Subsidy up to ₹12,00,000
  • Modernization of fishing vessels
  • Safety equipment for fishermen
  • Training and capacity building

Inland Fisheries Development

Construction of new ponds/tanks, renovation of existing ponds/tanks and community tanks for freshwater aquaculture.

Subsidy up to 40% of project cost
  • Construction of new ponds and tanks
  • Input support for fish farming
  • Integrated fish farming

Biofloc Technology

Support for biofloc technology adoption for intensive fish and shrimp farming with minimal water usage.

Subsidy up to 40% for general and 60% for SC/ST/Women
  • Biofloc units of various sizes
  • Equipment support (aerators, blowers)
  • Technical training and support

Ornamental Fisheries

Development of ornamental fish breeding and culture with focus on export potential.

Subsidy up to 40% for general and 60% for SC/ST/Women
  • Backyard ornamental fish rearing units
  • Medium-scale ornamental fish breeding units
  • Integrated ornamental fish units

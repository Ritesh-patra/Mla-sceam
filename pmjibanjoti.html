Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

A government-backed life insurance scheme aimed at providing financial security to families in case of the policyholder’s death.

Key Features

  • Life insurance cover of ₹2,00,000 in case of policyholder's death.
  • Annual premium of only ₹436.
  • Coverage valid for one year and renewable every year.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Any Indian citizen aged between 18 and 50 years.
  • Must have a savings bank account.
  • Aadhaar card must be linked with the bank account.
  • Premium is auto-debited from the bank account annually.

How to Claim?

In case of the policyholder's death, the nominee should visit the bank branch where the policy was enrolled. They need to submit the death certificate, filled claim form, and identity documents to initiate the claim process. Upon approval, the claim amount of ₹2,00,000 is transferred to the nominee’s bank account.

Important Information

The scheme offers compensation up to ₹2,00,000 for road accidents. Coverage includes medical expenses, disability benefits, and family support. The program is operated by Union Bank of India under the Centre for Financial Literacy (CFL) initiative.

Eligibility Criteria

Check if you qualify for our road accident compensation scheme

Who Can Apply?

  • Indian citizens aged 18-70 years
  • Vehicle owners and regular commuters
  • Valid identification and address proof
  • Bank account with Union Bank of India or any other scheduled bank

Required Documents

  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID)
  • Address Proof (Utility Bill, Rental Agreement)
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Bank Account Details
  • Vehicle Registration Documents (if applicable)

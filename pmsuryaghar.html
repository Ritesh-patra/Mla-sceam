Eligibility Criteria

Who is PM Surya Ghar Yojana For?

The household must be an Indian citizen

Pre-installed meter must be on individual's name

Roof must be suitable for installing solar panels

Household must have a valid electricity connection

What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a government scheme launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2024 to encourage the use of solar power. This initiative aims to install rooftop solar panels in 1 crore households, offering up to 300 units of free electricity per month. The scheme helps reduce electricity bills and promotes green energy while saving the government approx. ₹75,000 crore per year.

Key Benefits

  • Lower Electricity Bills: Specially benefits households consuming up to 300 units monthly.
  • Energy Self-sufficiency: Promotes rooftop solar to reduce dependence on external electricity sources.
  • Economic Relief: Reduces bills, helping families allocate budget to other needs.
  • Empowerment: Gives people control over energy use and increases independence.

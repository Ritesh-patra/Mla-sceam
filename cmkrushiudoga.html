The government is providing financial assistance and support for poultry farming to promote entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihoods in rural areas.

About the Scheme

The Poultry Farming Assistance Scheme is designed to support individuals interested in establishing or expanding poultry farming operations. The scheme provides financial assistance, technical guidance, and marketing support to ensure sustainable livelihoods and promote entrepreneurship in rural areas.

Financial Assistance

  • • Subsidy of ₹30,000/- per unit (for 50 birds)
  • • ₹40,000/- per unit (for 100 birds or more)
  • • Additional support for feed and equipment

Eligibility Criteria

  • • Permanent resident of the state
  • • Age between 18-60 years
  • • Priority to women, SC/ST, and other disadvantaged groups
  • • Must have suitable land for poultry farming

How to Apply

Applications can be submitted online through the official portal (www.sugam.odisha.gov.in) or at the nearest District Industries Center/MSME office.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for this scheme?

Any permanent resident of the state between 18-60 years of age can apply. Priority is given to women, SC/ST candidates, and other disadvantaged groups.

How much financial assistance is provided?

The scheme provides a subsidy of ₹30,000 per unit for 50 birds and ₹40,000 per unit for 100 birds or more. Additional support for feed and equipment may also be available.

Is training provided under this scheme?

Yes, beneficiaries will receive training on poultry farming techniques, disease management, and marketing strategies from experts in the field.

How long does the application process take?

The application process typically takes 30-45 days from submission to approval, depending on document verification and site inspection.

Can I apply if I already have a small poultry farm?

Yes, existing poultry farmers can apply for expansion or modernization of their farms under this scheme.
